New Tab Sound Candidates

Nineteen short synthesized options for the new-tab action. The first set is tuned specifically as a quiet confirmation that a new tab has opened.

Recommended For Tab Open

Open Rise newtab-open-rise.wav

Soft upward confirmation, clean and calm. My first pick for “new tab opened”.

Open Halo newtab-open-halo.wav

A rounded bell-like cue with a smooth tail. More elegant, still understated.

Open Zen newtab-open-zen.wav

A gentle two-step resolve. Very soft, with less sparkle than the bell options.

Open Drop newtab-open-drop.wav

Compact, delicate, and slightly brighter. Good if the cue should be very short.

Open Whisper newtab-open-whisper.wav

The quietest option: a soft breath plus a small tonal lift.

Softer System Set

Luna Bell newtab-soft-luna-bell.wav

Soft bell partials with a calm tail. This is the closest to a polished system notification.

Soft Bloom newtab-soft-bloom.wav

A rounded three-note bloom. Gentle, friendly, and not too attention-seeking.

Pearl Drop newtab-soft-pearl-drop.wav

A quiet high chime with a warm landing. Elegant and very compact.

Quiet Resolve newtab-soft-quiet-resolve.wav

Two soft tones that resolve upward. Calm, subtle, and suitable for repeated use.

Mist Chime newtab-soft-mist-chime.wav

Airy, restrained, and smooth. A softer chime with a little breath around it.

Velvet Pop newtab-soft-velvet-pop.wav

A pillowy tap with a soft tone after it. Best if you want a little physical feedback.

Original Set

Glass Ping newtab-glass-ping.wav

Bright, clean, and lightweight. Feels polished without sounding like a system alert.

Soft Pop newtab-soft-pop.wav

Warm, rounded, and friendly. Good if the tab action should feel tactile but gentle.

Card Flick newtab-card-flick.wav

A quick paper-and-card motion, matching the deck metaphor more directly.

Air Swipe newtab-air-swipe.wav

A soft whoosh with a small chime tail. Best for a more spacious, animated feel.

Pixel Blip newtab-pixel-blip.wav

Short, crisp, and playful. The most obviously digital option.

Deck Click newtab-deck-click.wav

Dry, physical, and compact. A good fit if you want the click to feel intentional.

Magnetic Snap newtab-magnetic-snap.wav

A snappy, slightly futuristic confirmation sound with a quick upward pull.

Page Turn newtab-page-turn.wav

Quiet rustle plus a tiny landing tap. The least intrusive candidate.
